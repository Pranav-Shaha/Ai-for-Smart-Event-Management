Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EventOps AI collects, uses, and protects information when you use our event management platform.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, register for an event, submit feedback, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, account credentials, event registrations, feedback, and the contents of messages you send to us.

We also collect information needed to operate the platform, such as account activity, event-management records, and basic technical information sent by your browser.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • create and maintain your account;
  • process event registrations and provide event-management features;
  • respond to questions, support requests, and feedback;
  • improve the reliability, security, and functionality of the platform; and
  • detect, prevent, and address misuse or unauthorized access.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only when it is necessary to provide the requested service, operate and secure the platform, comply with a legal obligation, or protect the rights and safety of EventOps AI and its users.

4. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

EventOps AI may use essential cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, maintain sessions, and support core platform functionality. Your browser may allow you to manage or block cookies, though some features may not work correctly without them.

6. Your choices and rights

You may review and update certain account information through the platform. You may also contact us to ask about your personal information, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Children’s privacy

The platform is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the platform after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please use our Contact page.
